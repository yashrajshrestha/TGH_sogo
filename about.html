A Home in the Heart of Thamel

Thamel Grand Hotel was born from a simple observation. As Nepal’s mountains drew more and more trekkers and travelers from around the world, Thamel — Kathmandu’s beating heart of tourism — filled with visitors from every corner of the globe. Watching this steady rise of travelers pass through our streets, our family saw not just an opportunity, but a calling: to give every guest who lands in Kathmandu a place that feels like home before they head into the mountains, and a place they long to return to after.

Our story

Family-run since 2014

  1. 2014

    A family opens its doors

    Thamel Grand Hotel is founded as a family-run business, built on a personal vision — to help shape Nepal’s tourism into something more welcoming, more genuine, and more memorable than what came before.

  2. Where you stay matters

    In the heart of Thamel

    Steps away from the markets, cafés, trekking agencies and energy that make this neighbourhood famous — while still offering a calm, comfortable retreat to return to at the end of the day.

  3. More than a stay

    Known by name, not room number

    Our staff take pride in getting to know every guest. Many tell us that by the time they check out, our team feels less like hotel staff and more like family. That warmth is not an accident; it’s the standard we hold ourselves to.

  4. Every year

    Comfort that doesn’t stand still

    We renovate and upgrade our rooms and amenities each year, listening closely to guest feedback to make sure every stay is better than the last.

  5. Today

    Over a decade of welcoming travellers

    More than ten years on, that first vision still guides everything we do — a home in Kathmandu before the mountains, and a place to come back to after.
