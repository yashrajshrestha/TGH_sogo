Our story

A warm base in the heart of Thamel

Thamel Grand Hotel grew from a simple idea: give travellers a comfortable, welcoming home in the middle of Kathmandu’s most vibrant quarter. Thamel has long been the gateway for trekkers, pilgrims and explorers heading into the Himalaya — and we wanted a place that matched its energy with genuine care and calm.

Guests from around the world stay with us for the easy location, the warm service, and the rooftop Black Olives Café & Bar, where breakfast, hearty meals and evening drinks come with a view over the neighbourhood.

Whether you’re resting between treks, discovering the Kathmandu Valley, or simply soaking up Thamel, we’re here to make your stay easy and memorable.
