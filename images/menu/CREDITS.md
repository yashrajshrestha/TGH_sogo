# Menu highlight photos — source & licence

Stock food photography, **not** photographs of Black Olives Cafe's own plating.
Every file was chosen from Wikimedia Commons under **CC0 or public domain** only:
free to use commercially, free to modify, no attribution required. Credits are
kept here anyway as provenance.

Backgrounds were removed on-device (macOS Vision subject lifting) and each shot
was cropped to the dish, resized to 560px and saved as WebP.
